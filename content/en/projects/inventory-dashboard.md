Full-stack system built for SPPG Sinar Asri (Dec 2025 - Mar 2026) to manage the budget and goods of a catering kitchen operating under the MBG program.

## Backend

- **Go, Fiber, GORM, PostgreSQL** with 35 REST API endpoints and role-based access control.
- Finance and raw-material inventory modules protected by **row-level locking** to prevent race conditions.

## Frontend

- **React, TypeScript, TanStack Query** with 8 functional pages and Recharts analytics dashboards visualizing financial and stock data.
